## My Learning Experience

When I first started exploring a career in data science, I spent a lot of time learning Python in great detail. At the time, I did not have a mentor to guide me, so I kept studying without knowing which topics I would actually need at the beginning.

Later, I looked more closely at the data science job market and realized that I had spent time learning many things I did not need yet. I also forgot some of what I had studied because I was not using it in practice.

Here is what I learned from that experience:

1. **Start with the Python basics you need.** Focus on the concepts that help you begin working with data.
2. **Learn by applying what you study.** Practicing Python as you explore data science can make it easier to remember.
3. **Build on your knowledge over time.** As your data science career develops, you can learn more Python when your work requires it.
4. Don’t aim for perfection in everything you learn. Spending too much time perfecting one skill can slow your progress. Once you’ve learned the essentials, move forward **done beats perfect, every single time.**

## A Note for Beginners

You do not need to master all of Python before beginning your data science journey. A focused foundation can be enough to get started, and you can learn additional concepts at the right time as your skills and experience grow.

Trying to learn everything at once may feel overwhelming, and you may forget topics you do not use. My experience taught me to learn what I need for the current step, practise it, and keep moving forward.

I hope this repository helps you make a confident start. Learn step by step, keep practising, and remember: **the right knowledge at the right time can make your learning journey more effective.**
